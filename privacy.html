CyberEthic ("we", "us", or "our") operates the CyberEthic: AI Social Commenter & Growth Assistant browser extension ("Extension"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information processed through our Extension in strict compliance with the Google Chrome Web Store Developer Program Policies.

Key Summary: We never sell your personal data. We only process feed text you choose to evaluate to generate AI comments, and store your license and personal settings securely.

1. Information We Collect

To provide you with context-aware AI comment generation and licensing services, the Extension accesses and processes the following information:

  • Account & Licensing Data: When activating a CyberEthic license or premium tier, we collect your email address and license key. This is used solely to authenticate your account and verify subscription entitlements.
  • Social Media Content (Ephemeral): When you activate scanning on supported platforms (LinkedIn, X / Twitter), the Extension reads the visible post text within your active viewport in order to evaluate its context and craft a relevant reply.
  • Local User Preferences: Your configured settings (e.g., target keywords, negative keywords, preferred tone, auto-like toggles, and daily cooldown settings) are stored locally in your browser via chrome.storage.local.
  • Local Engagement History: Anonymized IDs of posts you have engaged with are saved locally in your browser storage to prevent the Extension from commenting on the same post twice.

Information We Never Collect: We do NOT collect or store your social media account passwords, banking/payment card details, private direct messages (DMs), contact lists, or browsing activity outside of supported active tabs.

2. How We Use and Process Information

We use the collected information exclusively for the following specific purposes:

  • To verify user license keys and activate product features.
  • To submit post text to secure AI language model endpoints to generate contextual, human-like comment drafts.
  • To enforce user-defined daily limits, cooldown intervals, and spam-prevention rules locally on your browser.

3. Data Storage, Retention, and Security

  • Transient AI Processing: Visible post text transmitted for AI generation is processed in-memory in real time. We do not store, log, or build behavioral profiles from your feed content on our servers.
  • Local Storage: Your configuration, engagement logs, and preferences reside solely on your device within your browser's private extension storage.
  • Encryption in Transit: All communications between the Extension, our licensing API (api.cyberethic.in), and AI compute gateways (api.kilo.ai) are encrypted using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS).

4. Data Sharing and Third-Party Disclosures

We strictly comply with the Google Chrome Web Store User Data Policy:

  • No Sale of Data: We do never sell, trade, or rent your personal or browsing data to any third parties, advertisers, or data brokers.
  • Single Purpose Rule: Data is accessed solely to perform the stated core function of the Extension: AI-assisted social commentary and engagement.
  • AI Gateway Providers: When generating comment drafts, text is processed through secure, enterprise-grade AI infrastructure under strict confidentiality terms prohibiting the use of your data for model training or secondary purposes.
  • No Credit or Lending Uses: We do not use or transfer user data to determine creditworthiness or for financial lending purposes.

5. User Rights, Data Control, and Deletion

  • Clearing Local Data: You can delete all locally stored preferences, comment histories, and cached credentials at any time by clearing your browser's extension data or uninstalling the Extension.
  • Account & Email Deletion: If you wish to delete your email address and license records from our licensing database, you may submit a deletion request to support@cyberethic.in, and your data will be permanently removed within 3 business days.

6. Permissions Justification

In accordance with Chrome Web Store least-privilege principles, the Extension only requests essential permissions:

  • activeTab & tabs: To interact with the active social media tab (LinkedIn / X) when you choose to run the assistant.
  • scripting: To inject content scripts that display the on-screen dashboard and execute user-authorized comments.
  • storage: To save your keywords, settings, and cooldown states locally in your browser.
